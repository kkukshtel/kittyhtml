KITTYHTML · DEMO
HTML, in your terminal.
Rendered by DropFlow, displayed via the Kitty graphics protocol.
No Chromium, no headless browser. Just CSS layout to a PNG, base64-streamed into your terminal as one inline image. Useful when an AI agent has something to show you that reads better as a page than as plain text.
What this is good for
•  Inline reports, tables, and summaries from agents.
•  Quick previews of email or marketing copy.
•  Lightweight diagrams that compose better in HTML than in ASCII.
$ echo '<h1>hi</h1>' | kittyhtml
Tip: try --scale 2 for sharper text on high-DPI displays.
