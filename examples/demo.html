KITTYHTML · v0.3

HTML, in your terminal.

Rendered by Blitz, displayed via the Kitty graphics protocol.

No Chromium, no headless browser. Just a Rust + Vello renderer producing a PNG, base64-streamed into your terminal as one inline image. Useful when an AI agent has something to show you that reads better as a page than as plain text.

What this is good for

  • Inline reports, tables, and summaries from agents.
  • Quick previews of email or marketing copy.
  • Lightweight diagrams that compose better in HTML than ASCII.
